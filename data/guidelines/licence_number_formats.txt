GUIDE TO BIS LICENCE AND REGISTRATION NUMBER FORMATS

Understanding the different BIS number formats helps quickly identify what kind of certification a product carries and whether the format is genuine.

1. ISI MARK LICENCE - CM/L FORMAT
Format: CM/L-XXXXXXXXXX where X are digits (7 to 10 digits).
Example: CM/L-8700123456.
Found printed below the ISI mark on certified products such as cement bags, TMT bars, LPG cylinders, helmets and electrical appliances. The CM/L number identifies the licensed manufacturer and the specific Indian Standard.

2. CRS REGISTRATION - R NUMBER
Format: R-XXXXXX (usually six to eight digits).
Example: R-61001234.
Used for electronics and IT goods registered under the Compulsory Registration Scheme: LED lamps, mobile phones, laptops, power banks, adapters. The R-number must appear on the product along with the Standard Mark.

3. HALLMARK UNIQUE IDENTIFICATION - HUID
Format: six character alphanumeric code.
Example: H9X2AB.
Laser marked on every hallmarked gold jewellery item. Buyers can verify it instantly using the BIS Care mobile application. Hallmarking is mandatory in notified districts for gold jewellery sales.

4. FOREIGN MANUFACTURERS CERTIFICATION SCHEME - FML
Format: FML-XXXXXXXXXX (6 to 10 digits).
Example: FML-1234567890.
Issued to foreign manufacturers who export certified goods to India. Requires appointment of an authorized Indian representative and a factory audit by BIS officials.

HOW TO VERIFY
Enter the number exactly as printed (including prefixes) in the licence verification section of bis.gov.in or the BIS Care app. A valid format does not guarantee authenticity; always confirm holder name, product scope and licence status (active, suspended or cancelled) through official channels.

WARNING SIGNS OF FAKE CERTIFICATION
Missing CM/L or R-number next to a printed ISI mark.
Numbers that do not match the official format pattern.
Holder name mismatch when verified on the portal.
Blurry or tampered marks on packaging.
